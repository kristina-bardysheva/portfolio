About Kristina — ASO and App Store conversion designer

Kristina is a senior mobile app designer who works at the point where design meets app growth. Her core job is the App Store and Google Play product page: she turns marketing goals, audience insight and ASO analytics into screenshot systems and store creatives that explain an app in seconds and persuade a visitor to install. She treats every creative as a hypothesis to be tested, not a portfolio piece.

  • ASO creative strategy: audience research, competitor and category analysis, messaging hierarchy and creative hypotheses for the store page.
  • App Store screenshot systems: first-screenshot storytelling, bold captions, real UI on devices, consistent visual rhythm across the set.
  • AI-powered creative design: generating AI portraits, models and styles with Midjourney, Adobe Firefly and Hailuo AI, then art-directing them into polished store visuals.
  • Mobile app growth design: aligning visuals with conversion goals for photo-editing, AI, travel and screen-time apps.
  • Supporting craft: UI and product presentation, visual identity, 3D mascots (Cinema 4D, Octane), motion (After Effects), Figma.

How Kristina works: from growth goal to store creative

  1. Goal and audience. Starts from the marketing objective and the target audience: what the app must communicate, to whom, and what the category's visitors expect.
  2. Analysis. Reviews ASO analytics, behavioral patterns and competitor store pages, and audits the existing screenshots (several projects are redesigns with before/after comparison).
  3. Hypotheses. Defines concrete creative hypotheses: dark vs. light background, hero feature on screenshot 1, mascot-led vs. photo-led, before/after storytelling, caption style.
  4. Design. Builds the full screenshot set as one visual story with a clear hierarchy, real UI on realistic devices, and AI-generated or art-directed imagery for different target audiences.
  5. Iteration. Refines creatives against performance data and ships variants for testing.
